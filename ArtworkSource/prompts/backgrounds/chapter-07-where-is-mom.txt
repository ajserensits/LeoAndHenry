Chapter 7, "Where Is Mom?" The front garden and winding lane of the cottage in warm late-afternoon light, a little larger and more open than the backyard. The calm center is a soft cobblestone path winding away between low lavender hedges toward the cottage's front door, with a faint trail of tiny crumbs. Far-left margin: Leo sits small and uncertain beside a garden lantern, looking around for Mom. Far-right margin: Henry sniffs a well-loved pink slipper lying by a watering can and a garden bench, nose to the ground, following the clue. In a far background window of the cottage, a warm soft silhouette of a woman with no visible face, glowing and welcoming.
