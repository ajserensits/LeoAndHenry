Chapter 2, "The Vacuum Awakens." Inside a cozy cottage on cleaning day, seen from a small dog's eye level, warm morning sunlight. The calm center is a long sunlit wooden hallway floor with a woven runner rug leading to an open back door that glows with green garden light far away. Far-left margin: Leo peeks around a doorframe behind a laundry basket, a stolen dog biscuit in his mouth, eyes wide and nervous. Far-right margin: a chunky rounded sage-green upright vacuum with a coiled cord stands parked beside a potted fern, and Henry sits in front of it, alert, looking across toward Leo protectively. Dust motes sparkle in the light.
