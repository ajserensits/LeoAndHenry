Chapter 3, "Under the Bed." A cozy cottage bedroom at dusk, seen from floor level, warm lamplight and soft amber glow, safe and never spooky. The calm center is a soft round braided rug on a wooden floor receding toward a window with a gentle sunset sky and sheer curtains. Far-left margin: the low gap under a big quilted patchwork bed with a dangling fringe; Leo is squished underneath, only his round fluffy face, big worried eyes, and one paw peeking out. Far-right margin: Henry lies flat on his belly beside a nightstand with a glowing lamp, chin on his paws, looking patiently toward Leo. A red plush ladybug toy lies on the floor near the left margin.
